Real garden photos are harder than lab photos, so always check the top 3 guesses. When the AI is less than 50% sure, it says "Not sure" instead of guessing. Care tips are general guidance, not a substitute for an agronomist or plant pathologist.

How it works

The detector uses an EfficientNetV2 convolutional neural network built with TensorFlow, trained with transfer learning on the PlantVillage dataset (about 54,000 lab photos of leaves) plus the PlantDoc dataset (about 2,000 real-world photos). It runs on Hugging Face Spaces with a Gradio interface. The training notebooks and code are open source on GitHub.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It's completely free and needs no account. It works in any web browser (Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge) on phones and computers.

Are my photos stored?

No. Photos are only used to make the prediction and are deleted from the server within an hour. Uploads are limited to 10 MB. We count visits and scans anonymously (no cookies, no personal data, never the photo) to see how the tool is used.

What if my plant isn't on the list?

The AI only knows the 14 crops above. For other plants it will usually say "Not sure" or give a wrong guess.

Who made it?

It's a student capstone project in machine learning, built from data cleaning and model training through to a public website.
